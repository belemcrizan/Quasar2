# Adversarial review

1. **External validity is not demonstrated.** NASA/INSPIRE sources are real, but
   queries are generated from indexed identity/title fields. They do not replace
   native queries, human intent annotation, difficult WDI tuples or unseen domains.
2. **The real-source gate degenerates to STOP.** Matching a zero-action quota makes
   all matched baselines identical. A zero-width interval here is a design
   degeneracy, not evidence of precise general superiority.
3. **The synthetic benefit is conditional.** R* beats selected matched comparators
   in this control, but its primary lower bounds do not exceed the preregistered
   minimum effect .01, and ALWAYS has greater unconstrained utility at price .05.
4. **Proxy features may be weak or tautological.** Coverage-derived recoverability,
   correlated uncertainty features and constant cost cannot establish the intended
   causal mechanism. No feature independence is claimed.
5. **No strong neural comparison ran.** Hashing retrieval is explicitly a debug
   proxy. Optional existing neural backends are not new empirical evidence.
6. **Integrity is not experimental independence.** Local hashes, syntax checks and
   lexical screens cannot certify unseen model training corpora or annotation
   independence. The first-500 near-duplicate screen is incomplete by design.
7. **Compute budgets are only partly matched.** Retrieval counts match exactly;
   model tokens, latency and index construction are outside that matching claim.
8. **Not paper-ready.** Human annotation, native IR, WDI-Hard, domain/cost/model
   transfer, live agents, scale and Docker remain missing. A polished manuscript
   cannot substitute for these experiments. No 0.3 release is justified.
